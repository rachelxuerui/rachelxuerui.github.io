    Apartment in Sutton Place

A project for a friend.

The apartment has a dining room window that looks onto the Queensboro Bridge at the level of traffic. To intensify the view's effect, a pair of existing double-hung windows are replaced with a large pane of glass. Next to the new picture window, a wall separating the dining room and the kitchen is clad with aluminum panels to reflect diffused lights from the bridge at night. Behind the aluminum wall, a new kitchen is made of hand-polished McMaster-Carr parts, strung together by custom 3D-printed fittings, and topped with polished marble.

As of , the project is complete, but the friend has since moved out of the country for another job. The apartment is currently on the market if anyone is interested.
